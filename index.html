Why these five

Open-plan noise, screen glare, restlessness and time-blindness are four of the most common difficulties neurodivergent adults describe at work. Each product answers one of them with an ordinary object, chosen for its certification and build rather than for claims. We say what a product does and what it is tested to, and no more.
