For employers

Small, practical tools for the desks where work is harder than it needs to be.

Open-plan noise, harsh lighting, long stretches at a screen and time that slips away are everyday obstacles for many employees, and for neurodivergent colleagues in particular. Galene supplies discreet, adult desk tools that deal with each of these, with the compliance paperwork an employer needs to approve them.

Questions employers ask

What does Galene supply to employers?

Five desk tools, one job each: noise-reducing ear inserts, tinted focus glasses, a screen light bar, a weighted lap blanket and a visual timer. They deal with open-plan noise, glare, restlessness and losing track of time, and are supplied with the compliance paperwork an employer needs to approve them.

Who are the products for?

Adults at work. They were chosen with neurodivergent employees in mind, and any employee who finds noise, lighting or time hard to manage at a desk can use them.

Are these medical devices?

No. They are everyday desk tools. They are not medical devices and we make no claim that they treat or improve any condition. The ear inserts are certified hearing protection, and the attenuation we state is the certified figure.

Can they be used as a reasonable accommodation?

Employers in Ireland and across the EU have a duty to provide reasonable accommodation, and most requests are small: less noise, softer light, a way to see time passing. These tools are meant for exactly that. Whether a particular adjustment is right for an employee is for the employer and the employee to decide.

What paperwork comes with the products?

Each product is supplied with its declaration of conformity and a one-page specification for your health and safety or procurement file. The regulations and standards for every product are listed publicly at galene.ie/doc.

How much does it cost?

Pilot and volume prices are quoted on request, excluding VAT, on one invoice. Volume prices apply from 10 units. Email info@galene.ie with your employer name and approximate headcount for a quote.

Can we try the products before ordering?

Yes. We send one sample kit of the range to a named HR, occupational health or facilities contact to assess. Kits ship from March 2027, when our Irish stock arrives, and can be reserved now.

How does a pilot work?

A pilot is 10 to 25 units in any mix, for one site, team or employee network, at a fixed price on one invoice. After six weeks we send a short anonymous survey and share the results with you.

When are the products available?

The range launches on Amazon.de in January 2027. Employer sample kits ship from March 2027. The visual timer is planned for early 2027 and is not yet on sale.
